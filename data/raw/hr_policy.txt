Acme Corporation - Employee Leave Policy

1. Purpose
This policy describes the types of leave available to employees of Acme Corporation
and the process for requesting time off. It applies to all full-time and part-time
employees who have completed their probation period.

2. Annual Leave
Full-time employees are entitled to 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Annual leave accrues at a rate of 2 days per month. Unused leave of up to 5 days may
be carried over into the following year; any excess is forfeited on 31 December.

3. Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate
is required for any absence longer than two consecutive days. Sick leave does not
carry over between years.

4. Maternity Leave
Eligible employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. To qualify, an
employee must have completed at least 12 months of continuous service before the
expected date of delivery. Maternity leave may begin up to 4 weeks before the due
date. Employees must notify their manager and the HR department at least 8 weeks in
advance.

5. Paternity Leave
Employees are entitled to 2 weeks of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 8 weeks
of the birth or adoption of a child.

6. Requesting Leave
All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 working days in
advance, except in the case of emergency or illness. Managers are expected to approve
or decline requests within 2 working days.

7. Contact
For questions about this policy, contact the People Operations team at
hr@acme.example.
